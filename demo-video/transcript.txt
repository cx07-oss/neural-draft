01. THE IDEA
Meet Neural Draft. Apply a skill in a short simulation, forge a personal card, and put what you learned into play.

02. SOLO FORGE
The Solo Forge starts with a suspicious vendor update. Production is healthy, but an unfamiliar sender demands an urgent installation.

03. INVESTIGATE
Nova inspects the evidence and asks Leena how to verify the update. The signer is unexpected. Leena points to a trusted contact, independent of the email.

04. MAKE THE CALL
Nova pauses the rollout, preserves evidence, and chooses independent verification. A one sentence reason records the judgement behind that action.

05. THE FORGE
The decision becomes Nova's Relay Architect. Its Coordinate effect spreads influence between fronts, reflecting the choice to connect evidence, operations, and a trusted contact.

06. EXPLAINABLE ASSESSMENT
This offline demo scores the selected action and inspected evidence. It saves, but does not interpret, the written reason. Higher scores change appearance, never unrestricted power.

07. YOUR COLLECTION
The card and evidence stay in the local collection. Select a forged card and bring it, plus five starters, into a duel.

08. TWO PLAYERS
A second player joins with the room code. Quick demo provides an explicitly labelled practice card, so judges can reach the duel immediately.

09. THE TRANSFER TEST
The new case concerns an unusual file export. Across four rounds, both players compete for influence on Evidence, Response, and People.

10. SECRET COMMITMENT
Each player secretly chooses an unused card and a front. Nova locks the forged card. The rival sees only that the move is sealed.

11. APPLY IT AGAIN
Investigate requires choosing a relevant clue. Orion connects the export audit to the Evidence front. Applying the skill again unlocks the fixed bonus.

12. ROUND 01 / REVEAL
Both plays reveal together. The server awards the clue bonus and spreads Nova's coordination influence. Every score change has an explicit explanation.

13. ROUND 02 / ADAPT
In round two, Nova uses the live session clue to support Response. Orion contests that same front with Contain. Both earn their conditional bonuses.

14. ROUND 03 / ANTICIPATE
In round three, Nova's containment predicts the contested People front. Orion's Challenge finds no eligible counter. Tactical choices matter, with only four fixed effects.

15. ROUND 04 / RESULT
After round four, Nova leads two of the three fronts and wins. The result shows influential moves and a short learning insight based on the actual plays.

16. READY FOR ANOTHER ROUND
Both players can accept a rematch, while their collections remain saved. Neural Draft turns a learning decision into a skill you can use again.
